Trends: A much better political ad library

Who Targets Me Trends brings political ads from the platforms together into a single library, then does the time-consuming work of collecting, cleaning and structuring the data for you.

See who is advertising, what they’re spending, who they’re targeting and what they’re saying. Compare advertisers and campaigns, investigate trends and analyse political advertising at a scale that would take hours of manual work using the platform libraries.

Built for journalists, researchers, campaigners, agencies and civil society organisations, Trends turns millions of political ads, from hundreds of thousands of advertisers, in over 60 countries, into actionable intelligence.

  • A right of reply for digital ads

    Political parties spend enormous sums on digital ads, targeting voters with messages their opponents may never see, let alone challenge. Ad libraries have improved transparency, but they don’t allow opponents to respond directly to the audiences seeing those ads. What if parties and candidates had an automatic right of reply, displayed alongside the original advertisement?

  • Better regulation for digital political advertising in the UK

    The overall goal of political advertising reform and regulation in the UK should be to foster a transparent, trustworthy, diverse, and competitive political advertising market. Over more than a decade of study, our research has found that the benefits of digital political advertising generally outweigh the costs, but this gap could be (positively) widened with specific policy interventions.
